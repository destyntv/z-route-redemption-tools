⚙️ How Hits / Deaths Are Calculated (Simplified)

    
    
      
  • Army Morale = Soldiers × BaseMorale (~501.5) × (1 + MoraleBonus%)
    • 
      
  • Morale Ratio → Damage Coefficient (higher ratio = you deal more / take less)
    • 
      
  • Power ratio + faction bonus + position modifiers further scale the final loss %
    • 
      
  • Hospital capacity decides how many wounded become permanent deaths when full
    • 
      
  • Some events (certain Sieges) force 0 deaths – everything goes to hospital
    •

How the Kill Calculator works

    
    
      
  • Compares effective power (Hero + Soldier × tier multiplier) vs enemy.
    • 
      
  • Rally multiplies your side by the number of members (with diminishing returns after 5–6).
    • 
      
  • Higher troop tier reduces your losses and increases kill chance.
    • 
      
  • Hospital overflow turns wounded into permanent deaths.
    • 
      
  • This is a simplified model – real results also depend on formation, counters and RNG.

When to wait (double-dip VS + Mission Readiness)

    
    
      
  • Best rule: Only dump big speedups / shards / training when both the matching VS day and the matching Mission Readiness window are active.
    • 
      
  • Mission Readiness rotates categories in fixed daily windows (construction, train, research, heroes, fighter, etc.). Check in-game calendar for your server’s exact hours.
    • 
      
  • If VS is “Construction” but MR is “Train soldiers” right now → wait or do only cheap tasks; save build speedups until MR construction opens (or next Construction VS day).
    • 
      
  • Radar: stack missions before Monday; clear them on Radar day. Don’t burn the stack on a random day.
    • 
      
  • General speedups score on the day you use them, not when you bought them.
    • 
    


    
What to do while waiting

    
    
      
  • Claim free stamina / daily freebies that don’t spoil VS points.
    • 
      
  • Clear expiring radar (keep red-dot saved ones).
    • 
      
  • Heal lightly if hospital is full before a fight day.
    • 
      
  • Plan upgrade timers so they complete on the scoring day (start early enough).
    • 
      
  • Alliance chat: confirm hive / shield times for Raid day.
    • 
      
  • Use external: Route Reminder · Radar Calculator
    • 
    

    

      How zroute-tools.com gets your info: It does not log into the game or scrape your account. You type numbers from the game (radar level, missions on map, etc.). Values stay in your browser on that site — same model as this tool and zrouteops roster.

Pandora Event – Quick Guide

    
    
      
  1. Join when it opens; read rules (?); active alliance if required.
    2. 
      
  2. Basics – Complete quests for Pandora Points → unlock track rewards; rank matters.
    3. 
      
  3. Prepare early – Save speedups, resources, stamina, recruit tickets until matching tasks appear.
    4. 
      
  4. Play – Daily + special tasks on the right days; join alliance activities that score.
    5. 
      
  5. Claim – Milestones as you reach them; free dailies; rank rewards at end.
    6. 
      
  6. End – Claim everything; use expiring event items; start saving for next Pandora.
    7. 
    

    
Rewards often include hero shards, resources, speedups, equipment materials, and better guild rankings when the alliance participates together.

  


  

    
Defeat the Devourers – Quick Guide

    
    
      
  1. Find Devourers on the World Map via the event page.
    2. 
      
  2. Defeat them for Rogue Coins.
    3. 
      
  3. Spawn bosses – Kills can spawn Zombie Chiefs.
    4. 
      
  4. Rally with alliance on Zombie Chiefs.
    5. 
      
  5. Damage – More total boss damage → better ranking rewards.
    6. 
      
  6. Claim – Spend Rogue Coins in Event Shop before the event ends.
    7. 
    

    
Golden rule: No Devourers → no Zombie Chiefs → no big rewards. Kill Devourers to spawn chiefs. Use stamina, watch Alliance Discovery, join every chief rally you can.
